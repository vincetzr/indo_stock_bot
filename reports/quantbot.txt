  THREE ERRORS IN THIS CHECK BEFORE IT PASSED, ALL IN THE TEST:
   1. log increments of mean zero make the PRICE drift up by
      exp(sigma^2 T/2) = +5.2%; the labeller measured that and was
      blamed for it. Drift the logs by -sigma^2/2 instead.
   2. b/(a+b) is a PRICE-space formula; symmetric price barriers are
      asymmetric in log space (+0.276 against -0.382).
   3. feeding close=high=low forced every touch a day late and a
      whole daily move past the level. Simulating 20 intraday steps
      gives a true extreme and the overshoot collapses.
  The last of those is a SIMULATION artefact and is shown to be one
  by convergence — at the tightest geometry the error runs 0.0422 ->
  0.0330 -> 0.0135 -> 0.0114 -> 0.0033 as the intraday path is
  refined 1 -> 5 -> 20 -> 80 -> 320 steps. Real bars carry the TRUE
  intraday extreme, so on IDX the trigger is exact and this residual
  does not exist at all.
  ANY win rate is purchasable and buying it changes nothing: that is
  the whole constraint the goal runs into, now measured not asserted.
  the real-fill column is MORE negative than the symmetric one by 0.0027 on average: that gap is
  the modelled cost of a stop that fills at the close instead of at its level.
