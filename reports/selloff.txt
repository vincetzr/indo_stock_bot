  GIVE-BACK = median share of the in-trade peak handed back at the
  exit. It is the quantity the complaint is about.
  CAGR and HOLD are both compounded per name over the name's FULL
  span, so the rule is charged for the time it sits in cash. Scaling
  a 23-bar trade by 252/23 instead assumes it repeats back-to-back
  all year and overstates the rate ~3x.
  The two policies exist because the EDGE policy is the sensible
  trading rule and the LIVE policy is the fair experiment: a real
  detector fires on a drop that usually breaks the setup too, so it
  gets a fresh edge and rejoins the next leg, while the random arm
  sells mid-trend and is locked out until the trend dies and
  restarts. Read S3 (give-back) on either; read any CAGR comparison
  against the control on LIVE only.
