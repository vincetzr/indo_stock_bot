The trade being offered, in one sentence. You accept the possibility of losing more than half your money in order to earn about one and a quarter points a year over a rupiah time deposit — and only if you are an Indonesian taxpayer reinvesting dividends at home. A foreign holder is projected to lose to the deposit outright and should not run this book.

If that trade is not wanted, the deposit is the better answer. Saying so is the job.

01

What actually happened

Growth of Rp 1, log scale. The book is the top 30 IDX names by trailing dividend yield, equal weight, rebalanced once a year, dividends reinvested, 0.56% charged on every round trip.

02

What is certain

These are not forecasts. They are properties of the arithmetic, and they are where most of the achievable improvement lives.

Cost — 0.56% a round trip

Buy 0.28%, sell 0.18% plus the 0.1% sales tax. The only figure in this project guaranteed to be exactly what it says. Trading less is the one return improvement that cannot fail, which is why the book rebalances annually and not monthly: 0.19%/yr against 0.73%, for no measured difference in return.

Breadth — roughly fifteen points a year, for free

Books drawn at random from the eligible universe, so nothing here is a selection effect.

Income — 87% of liquid IDX names pay a dividend

Every study in this repository before this one read the raw close instead of the adjusted close, and therefore silently excluded every dividend ever paid. Correcting it lifts the equal-weight liquid book from 2.23%/yr to 4.26%/yr — a correction larger than any edge this project had ever claimed to find.

Tax — the exemption is worth more than most signals

Dividends reinvested in Indonesia are exempt under PP 9/2021. Otherwise 10% final for a domestic individual, 20% or the treaty rate for a foreign holder. On this book the exemption is worth about Rp 5.8m a year per Rp 1bn deployed — for filing a form.

And holding periods are a distribution, not a number

Of every five-year holding period in this universe, 36% were negative and 82% came in under the deposit rate. Money that might be needed inside five years does not belong in this book at all.

What was ruled out

05

What survived, and how hard it was pushed

Trailing dividend yield is computable point-in-time without any fundamental history: the adjusted-close to raw-close ratio is the accumulated dividend, and its trailing growth is knowable at the decision bar. The implied payouts were checked against the real ones first — BBCA Rp 337, BMRI Rp 475, TLKM Rp 227. They match.

Three things held against it

  • It is trailing, not forward. It ranks what was already paid. A company that paid well and then cuts still ranks high on the day the cut is announced, and the book will be holding it.
  • It is a value factor wearing an income name. Yield is dividend over price, so a name whose price halved ranks higher on an unchanged payout. Part of this is simply buying what has fallen.
  • Every name in the test universe still trades today. Zero delistings are present, so the backtest is a survivor's account and the real figure is lower.

08

When to abandon it

A rule with no exit is a rule that gets rationalised. These are committed to before any money moves, and the twice-daily job checks the last two automatically.

  1. The book underperforms the equal-weight liquid universe for three consecutive calendar years. In the sample it was behind in 3 of 10 and never twice running.
  2. The average trailing yield of the top 30 falls below the deposit rate. There would then be no income premium left to harvest. It is 6.8% today.
  3. The liquidity floor stops clearing 30 names, which would mean the book can no longer be built at the size it is being run.

Nothing else. Not a bad quarter, not a drawdown, not a headline.

09

What is still open, and what this does not claim

Broker flow has never been tested with enough data

That is a different statement from having been tested and failed, and the difference matters. The hypotheses are frozen and hashed (6b8e0a2c9d1f4e73) so they cannot be edited once the answer is visible, and the daily store accumulates. At 250 complete days on a name the protocol runs and this plan is revisited on the result. Until then the plan assumes nothing about it — which is the entire reason the hypotheses were frozen in advance.

What this plan does not claim

  • That the next eleven years resemble the last eleven. The sample holds one commodity cycle, one pandemic and one crash.
  • That the yield premium is permanent. It is a well-documented global effect that has also spent decades at a time not working.
  • Low risk. The drawdown is the same as everything else here.
  • That it beats the deposit rate by much: +1.24%/yr for a domestic reinvesting holder, and −0.12%/yr for a foreign one — which is to say, for them, it does not beat it at all.

What it does claim is that of everything tested here, this is the only construction that survived a Bonferroni correction, a walk-forward, a grid, a split-half, a skip-the-top, a year-by-year, a crash and a tax calculation — and that its two largest components, breadth and low turnover, are arithmetic rather than forecast and therefore cannot stop working.
